Our story / Palani, Tamil Nadu

A vision,
made to be worn.

Founded by Ramya Saravanan in 2026, VARNIYAM was created around a simple belief: a woman should never have to choose between comfort, elegance, and self-expression.

Rooted in Indian soul and open to contemporary form, the brand is a space for all the moods that make her who she is.

Ramya Saravanan
Founder, VARNIYAM
